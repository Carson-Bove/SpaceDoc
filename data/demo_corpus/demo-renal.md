Kidney stones typically cause colicky flank pain that may radiate to the groin. Blood in the
urine is common and can be detected with a urine dipstick.

Spaceflight increases kidney stone risk because bone demineralization raises urinary calcium
and crew fluid intake may fall.

Point-of-care ultrasound of the kidney can show hydronephrosis, which supports obstruction by a stone.

Fever together with flank pain may indicate infection behind an obstructing stone. This
combination needs urgent treatment.
